QUEST HOTEL
IT EQUIPMENT AND PROPERTY ACCOUNTABILITY TERMS

Purpose
These terms apply when an employee receives, uses, or is assigned IT equipment,
access credentials, supplies, or other company property. The employee should
review the item and report any existing damage or missing accessories before
signing the issuance record.

GENERAL TERMS (APPLY TO ALL ITEMS)

1. Use company property carefully, for its intended business purpose, and in
   accordance with IT and security instructions.
2. Do not lend, transfer, sell, alter, dismantle, or allow an unauthorized
   person to use assigned property without approval from the responsible
   department.
3. Keep equipment and credentials secure. Do not disclose passwords, PINs,
   access codes, or authentication prompts. Lock devices when unattended.
4. Promptly report loss, theft, suspected unauthorized access, damage, or
   malfunction to the IT department and the employee's supervisor. Do not
   attempt unauthorized repairs or data recovery.
5. Return the item, accessories, and any issued documentation when requested,
   when reassigned, or when employment ends. Return items in reasonable
   condition, allowing for ordinary wear from authorized use.
6. Do not store, copy, or disclose confidential company or guest information
   except as authorized. Follow company rules for data handling and disposal.
7. The employee is responsible for promptly cooperating with inventory checks
   and providing the item for inspection or maintenance when requested.
8. Damage, loss, or missing accessories will be reviewed based on the facts,
   applicable company policy, and applicable law. These terms do not authorize
   an automatic deduction from wages or predetermine liability.
9. The employee should ask the issuing department for clarification before
   signing if any item, serial number, condition, or accessory is incorrect.

CATEGORY-SPECIFIC TERMS

KEYCARD
- Keep the keycard in a secure place and use it only for the access assigned.
- Do not lend, duplicate, photograph for sharing, or allow another person to
  use the card to enter a controlled area.
- Do not mark, punch, bend, or otherwise alter the card.
- Report a lost, stolen, damaged, or non-working card immediately so access can
  be disabled or the card replaced.
- Return the keycard when access is no longer required or when requested.

MOBILES PHONES
- Use the phone and any company SIM or service plan in accordance with company
  policy; avoid unauthorized charges and do not change service or security
  settings without approval.
- Protect the phone with the approved screen lock and security updates. Do not
  jailbreak, root, or install unapproved software.
- Keep company data in approved applications and promptly report suspected
  loss, theft, compromise, or unusual charges.
- Return the phone, SIM (if requested), charger, case, and other recorded
  accessories. Follow IT instructions before removing company data or accounts.

PHONE
- These terms apply to phones recorded under the "Phone" category, including
  mobile or desk phones as described on the issuance record.
- Use the assigned phone and any associated line or SIM for authorized work.
  Do not change account, forwarding, or security settings without approval.
- Protect call records, contacts, messages, and any company information stored
  on or accessible through the phone.
- Report loss, theft, damage, service problems, or suspected compromise
  promptly; return the phone and recorded accessories when requested.

LAPTOPS
- Carry the laptop in a protective manner and do not leave it unattended in
  public or an unsecured vehicle.
- Use the approved charger and accessories. Keep the device updated and use
  approved security tools; do not disable encryption, endpoint protection, or
  other security controls.
- Do not install unauthorized applications, connect unapproved devices, or
  store company information in personal accounts or unapproved cloud services.
- Back up work only through approved company systems. Report loss, theft,
  damage, or suspicious activity immediately.
- Return the laptop, charger, bag, dock, and other accessories listed on the
  issuance record.

LAPTOP
- The same laptop handling, security, software, data, incident-reporting, and
  return requirements above apply to items recorded under "Laptop."
- Confirm the device identifier, condition, charger, and accessories on the
  issuance record before accepting the assignment.

DESKTOP PC
- Use the desktop PC only for authorized work. Do not open the case, relocate
  it, or change internal components without IT approval.
- Keep the computer and its ventilation areas clear; do not expose it to
  liquids, excessive heat, or other avoidable hazards.
- Do not disable security controls or install unapproved software or hardware.
- Protect the display, keyboard, mouse, and other recorded accessories.
- Report faults or damage promptly and return all assigned components when
  requested or when reassigned.

DESKTOP
- The same authorized-use, physical-care, security, software, and return
  requirements for desktop computers apply to items recorded under "Desktop."
- The issuance record should identify the assigned computer and any included
  monitor, keyboard, mouse, cables, or other accessories.

MONITOR
- Handle the monitor carefully; use the stand or approved mounting equipment.
- Do not open, modify, or attempt to repair the monitor. Keep liquids away and
  avoid pressure or impact to the screen.
- Use approved display cables and mounting changes only with IT authorization.
- Return the monitor, stand, power adapter, cables, and other recorded
  accessories when requested.

PERIPHERALS & NETWORKING
- Use peripherals and networking equipment only for authorized business
  purposes and connect them only to approved devices or networks.
- Do not change network configuration, credentials, firmware, cabling, or
  security settings without IT authorization.
- Protect adapters, docks, keyboards, mice, cables, access points, switches,
  and other recorded components from loss or damage.
- Do not connect unknown or personally owned networking equipment to company
  networks without approval.
- Report faults, missing components, or suspected unauthorized connections
  promptly and return all assigned items when requested.

SECURITY & ACCESS CONTROL
- Use assigned security and access-control equipment only for the approved
  purpose and locations.
- Keep credentials, keys, tokens, and access devices secure; do not share,
  copy, or bypass access controls.
- Do not change configurations, permissions, recordings, or access lists
  without authorization.
- Report loss, tampering, malfunction, or suspected unauthorized access
  immediately so protective action can be taken.
- Return all equipment, keys, tokens, and accessories when access is no longer
  required or when requested.

CONSUMABLES, PARTS & SUPPLIES
- Use issued consumables and supplies for authorized work and in a reasonable
  manner that avoids unnecessary waste.
- Do not remove, transfer, or distribute stock for personal use or to another
  location without approval and an appropriate inventory record.
- Store parts and supplies safely and follow handling instructions where
  provided.
- Report shortages, damaged stock, incorrect quantities, or defects promptly.
- Record usage, returns, or stock transfers as required by the department.

OTHER OR NEW CATEGORIES
- Apply the General Terms above and follow any written handling instructions
  supplied with the item.
- Before issuing a new type of item, document its identifier, condition,
  accessories, permitted use, and any item-specific handling or return
  instructions on the issuance record.
